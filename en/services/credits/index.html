How do credits work?

Credits are used to unlock Dev Center plans. The easiest way to earn them is by boosting the Discord server.

Discord boostsCredits earned
1 boost300 credits
2 boosts600 credits
3 boosts900 credits
4 boosts1,200 credits

It’s simple: every active boost on the Dev Center server is worth 300 credits. Credits are awarded automatically for as long as the boost stays active on the server.
